DATASTAGE DIMENSION UNIT: customer dimension (SCD Type 2), maintained from successive full snapshots.
Three parallel jobs, orchestrated by the master sequence Seq_Retail_DW_Master as follows:
  1. J02_Land_Customers stages the 2010 and the 2011 customer snapshot files.
  2. J05_Dim_Customer_Initial loads version 1 of dim_customer from the 2010 snapshot. The sequence runs it only
     when dim_customer does not exist yet (act_Check_Dim_Customer).
  3. J06_Dim_Customer_SCD2 applies the 2011 snapshot to the current dimension rows: unchanged customers are carried,
     changed customers are expired and get a new version, new customers are inserted. It writes dim_customer_next
     and audit_scd2_customer.
  4. act_Promote_Dim_Customer then replaces dim_customer with dim_customer_next (a file move).
Snapshot order: the 2010 snapshot is version 1, the 2011 snapshot is version 2.
The three job descriptions follow.
